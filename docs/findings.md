# Business Findings

## 1. Revenue Growth

Revenue increased substantially between 2012 and 2014.

| Year | Revenue | Orders | Average Order Value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2012 | $129,274.14 | 2,586 | $49.99 |
| 2013 | $393,247.87 | 7,447 | $52.81 |
| 2014 | $1,075,612.19 | 16,860 | $63.80 |
| 2015 | $340,375.55 | 5,420 | $62.80 |

2014 was the strongest year in the dataset, generating more than $1 million in revenue.

Average order value also increased from $49.99 in 2012 to $63.80 in 2014, indicating that customers were spending more per order.

---

## 2. Product Performance

The Original Mr. Fuzzy generated the highest revenue and net profit.

| Product | Gross Revenue | Net Profit | Net Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| The Original Mr. Fuzzy | $1,211,057.74 | $677,055.37 | 55.91% |
| The Forever Love Bear | $347,702.04 | $209,611.29 | 60.28% |
| The Birthday Sugar Panda | $229,260.15 | $143,184.51 | 62.46% |
| The Hudson River Mini Bear | $150,489.82 | $100,949.64 | 67.08% |

The Original Mr. Fuzzy is the largest profit contributor, while the Hudson River Mini Bear has the highest net profit margin.

This suggests that revenue volume and profitability efficiency are not necessarily the same.

---

## 3. Refund Performance

The business recorded 1,731 refund transactions totaling $85,338.69.

Gross revenue was $1,938,509.75, resulting in a refund rate of 4.40%.

After refunds, net revenue was $1,853,171.06.

Refund rates differed significantly by product:

| Product | Refund Rate |
|---|---:|
| The Birthday Sugar Panda | 6.04% |
| The Original Mr. Fuzzy | 5.11% |
| The Forever Love Bear | 2.23% |
| The Hudson River Mini Bear | 1.28% |

The Birthday Sugar Panda had the highest refund rate, while the Hudson River Mini Bear had the lowest.

---

## 4. Customer Retention

The dataset contains 31,696 customers, of which 591 placed more than one order.

This results in a repeat-customer rate of 1.86%.

The low repeat-customer rate suggests an opportunity to investigate customer retention and post-purchase engagement.

---

## 5. Device Performance

Desktop and mobile sessions showed a significant difference in conversion performance.

| Device | Sessions | Orders | Conversion Rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Desktop | 327,027 | 27,805 | 8.50% |
| Mobile | 145,844 | 4,508 | 3.09% |

Desktop converted at approximately 2.75 times the rate of mobile.

This suggests that the mobile purchasing experience could be investigated for usability or conversion barriers.

---

# Business Recommendations

### 1. Investigate mobile conversion

The large conversion gap between desktop and mobile suggests reviewing the mobile shopping journey, particularly product pages, cart, shipping, and checkout.

### 2. Investigate Birthday Sugar Panda refunds

The Birthday Sugar Panda has the highest refund rate at 6.04%.

The business could investigate refund reasons, product expectations, and customer feedback to identify potential issues.

### 3. Explore customer retention

With only 1.86% of customers placing multiple orders, retention represents a potential growth opportunity.

Customer segmentation, follow-up campaigns, and post-purchase engagement could be investigated.

### 4. Balance volume and margin

The Original Mr. Fuzzy generates the most total profit, while the Hudson River Mini Bear has the highest margin.

Business decisions should therefore consider both sales volume and profitability rather than focusing on revenue alone.
